The omens

    Bless a deploy

    Say what is shipping and the room bows while it goes out. First the shrine reads the omens: Friday, after 4 pm, a hungry kami, a full moon, Mercury retrograde. Shipped rings the bells and keeps the clean run going; It broke opens the vigil, named after the deploy. Nothing is ever stopped.

    Today’s forecast

    Every morning the kami reads the sky over your shrine, from Clear skies to Typhoon watch, one worse on a Friday, under a full moon or while Mercury is retrograde. A line about what it watches, a lucky offering, a lucky hour and one thing to avoid. The whole team reads the same one until midnight UTC.

    Draw a fortune

    An omikuji for engineers: five levels from Great blessing to Great curse, with a line about the deploy, the night on call and plain luck. One per person per vigil.

    Burn a blame

    Write what happened and burn it in the censer. The words never leave your browser; the room only learns that a confession was burned. Blameless, with a flame.
