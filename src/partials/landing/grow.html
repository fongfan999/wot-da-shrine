The shrine grows

Karma builds it. Bows raise it.

    Fed today

    Earn your mat

    Each level lays more mats, from one to twelve. The silk one and the cushions go to whoever earned the most karma this week; everyone past the mats stands at the back, and whoever holds the pager gets the on-call stool.

    Three bows raise it

    Karma brings the carpenters, not the roof. Scaffolding goes up, and three different people have to bow within a day to raise it. Bow in chorus, three within four seconds, and the bells ring for bonus karma. Every level opens a new sound for the room.

    Keep the kami fed

    Neglect shows on the building: dust after a day, cobwebs after two, weeds over the last two mats after four, and after a week a tanuki moves in. A shrine never shrinks.

    Hang your cloud’s lantern

    From the village shrine up, the clouds hang from the torii as donor lanterns. Pick yours and its lantern is lit, and the plaque reads its region. Heroku’s free tier hangs there too, dark, with a cobweb.

    And for the team: Keepers this week, a weekly vow, seventeen charms to collect, and credits that roll for everyone when the servers come back.
